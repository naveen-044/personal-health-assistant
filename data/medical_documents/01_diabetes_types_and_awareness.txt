# Type 1 and Type 2 Diabetes Awareness
Category: Chronic conditions
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## What diabetes means

Diabetes is a group of conditions that affect how the body regulates blood glucose. Type 1 and type 2 diabetes are different conditions. Type 1 involves the body's immune system damaging insulin-producing cells. Type 2 involves problems with insulin production or how the body uses insulin. This general description cannot identify which type a person has; testing and clinical assessment are needed.

## Symptoms and screening

Possible symptoms include increased thirst or urination, tiredness, blurred vision, or unexplained weight changes, but some people with type 2 diabetes have few noticeable symptoms. Symptoms alone cannot confirm diabetes. A healthcare professional can decide whether blood tests or screening are appropriate.

## Prevention and general care

Type 1 diabetes is not prevented by lifestyle choices. For type 2 diabetes, healthy eating, regular activity, and support from a healthcare team may help reduce risk. People already diagnosed should follow their individual care plan and should not change insulin or other medicines based on chatbot advice.

## When to seek help

Seek professional advice for possible symptoms, concerns about blood glucose, or questions about screening. Severe confusion, fainting, difficulty breathing, or rapidly worsening illness needs urgent medical assessment.
