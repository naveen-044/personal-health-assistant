# High Blood Pressure Awareness
Category: Chronic conditions
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Understanding blood pressure

High blood pressure, also called hypertension, often has no noticeable symptoms. A measurement is needed to identify it, and a clinician may consider more than one reading when assessing a person. One reading or a symptom checklist cannot establish a diagnosis.

## General prevention and care

Not smoking, regular physical activity, a balanced eating pattern, and limiting excess salt can support heart and blood-vessel health. Individual needs differ. A healthcare professional can explain blood-pressure readings, recommend follow-up, and discuss treatment if needed. Take prescribed medicines as directed; do not stop or change them based on chatbot advice.

## When to seek help

Arrange professional assessment for repeated high readings or concerns. Chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, sudden weakness, or new trouble speaking can signal an emergency. Contact local emergency services rather than waiting for online advice.
