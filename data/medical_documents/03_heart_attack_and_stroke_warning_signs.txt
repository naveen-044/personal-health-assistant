# Heart Attack and Stroke Warning Signs
Category: Heart and emergency awareness
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Possible heart attack warning signs

Heart attack symptoms can include chest discomfort or pressure, shortness of breath, discomfort in the upper body, nausea, or unusual cold sweat. Symptoms vary and may not look the same for everyone. Do not use this list to rule out an emergency.

## Possible stroke warning signs

Sudden face drooping, weakness or numbness (especially on one side), difficulty speaking or understanding, vision problems, loss of balance, or a sudden severe headache may be signs of stroke. Time matters: seek emergency care immediately, even if symptoms improve.

## What to do

Call local emergency services for possible heart attack or stroke symptoms. Do not drive yourself if emergency services are available, and do not delay for a chatbot response. Follow the emergency dispatcher’s instructions. Risk reduction and personal treatment decisions should be discussed with a healthcare professional.
