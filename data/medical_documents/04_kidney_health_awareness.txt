# Kidney Health Awareness
Category: Chronic conditions
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## What kidneys do

Kidneys filter waste and extra fluid from blood and help maintain the body's balance. Kidney disease may develop without obvious early symptoms. Diabetes and high blood pressure are important risk factors.

## Prevention and check-ups

Managing known health conditions with a healthcare team, avoiding smoking, and discussing kidney risk factors can support kidney health. A clinician can decide whether blood or urine tests are appropriate. Do not start supplements, non-prescription pain medicines, or major fluid changes to protect the kidneys without professional advice, especially if kidney disease is possible.

## When to seek help

Ask a healthcare professional about kidney screening if you have risk factors or persistent concerns. New swelling, changes in urination, blood in urine, or unexplained ongoing illness should be assessed. Severe shortness of breath, confusion, or rapid worsening needs urgent care.
