# Asthma Awareness
Category: Respiratory health
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## What asthma can involve

Asthma is a long-term condition affecting the airways. Symptoms can include wheezing, coughing, chest tightness, and shortness of breath. Symptoms and triggers differ between people. A clinician assesses symptoms and tests to determine whether asthma is present.

## General precautions

People with asthma should discuss an individual action plan and possible triggers with their healthcare professional. Use prescribed inhalers as instructed, keep follow-up appointments, and do not stop controller treatment because symptoms improve unless the prescriber advises it. Avoid smoke and other known irritants where practical.

## When to seek help

Severe breathing difficulty, blue or grey lips, inability to speak normally, or symptoms not improving with the person's prescribed emergency plan require urgent help. Contact local emergency services.
