# Thyroid Disorder Awareness
Category: Endocrine health
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## The thyroid and possible symptoms

The thyroid is a gland that makes hormones affecting many body functions. An underactive thyroid (hypothyroidism) can be associated with tiredness, feeling cold, dry skin, constipation, or other changes. These symptoms have many possible causes and do not prove a thyroid disorder.

## Assessment and precautions

A healthcare professional may assess symptoms and order blood tests. Do not start thyroid supplements or change prescribed thyroid medicine based on symptoms or chatbot advice. Ask the prescriber or pharmacist about medicine timing, interactions, and follow-up testing.

## When to seek help

Discuss persistent or concerning symptoms with a clinician. Seek urgent care for severe confusion, fainting, chest pain, or serious breathing problems.
