# Headache Awareness
Category: Symptoms
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## General information

Headache is a symptom with many possible causes, including common illnesses, stress, dehydration, or other health problems. The location or intensity alone does not identify a cause. Note when symptoms began and any associated symptoms to share with a clinician.

## General precautions

Rest, fluids, and avoiding a known trigger may be reasonable for some mild headaches, but needs vary. Ask a pharmacist or healthcare professional before taking pain medicine, particularly with other medicines, pregnancy, chronic illness, or for a child. Do not exceed package directions or combine products with the same active ingredient.

## When to seek help

Get urgent medical help for a sudden severe headache, headache after a serious injury, new weakness or confusion, vision loss, stiff neck with fever, or a rapidly worsening condition. Seek professional advice for recurring, persistent, or unusual headaches.
