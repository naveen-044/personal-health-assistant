# Migraine Awareness
Category: Symptoms
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## What migraine may feel like

Migraine is a type of headache disorder. Episodes may include moderate or severe head pain, nausea, and sensitivity to light or sound. Some people experience warning symptoms, but experiences vary. A clinician can assess recurring headaches and consider other causes.

## General precautions

Keeping a record of headache timing, symptoms, sleep, and possible triggers can help a healthcare professional. Regular meals, hydration, and a consistent sleep routine may support general wellbeing, but do not replace assessment or treatment. Do not start or change migraine medicines based on chatbot advice.

## When to seek help

Seek urgent help for sudden severe pain, new weakness, trouble speaking, confusion, fainting, or new vision loss. Discuss frequent, changing, or disabling headaches with a healthcare professional.
