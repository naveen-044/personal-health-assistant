# Fever, Cough, Fatigue, and Dizziness
Category: Common symptoms
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Understanding common symptoms

Fever can occur with infection and other conditions. A cough is a protective reflex and can accompany a cold, flu, asthma, or other problems. Fatigue means unusual tiredness or low energy and may relate to sleep, stress, illness, medicines, or other causes. Dizziness can mean lightheadedness, unsteadiness, or a spinning feeling. None of these symptoms alone identifies a disease.

## General precautions

Rest and drink fluids as appropriate for your circumstances. People with fluid restrictions or chronic conditions should follow their care team's advice. Reduce spread of respiratory illness with hand hygiene and cough etiquette. Ask a clinician or pharmacist before giving medicines to a child or combining products.

## When to seek help

Get urgent help for severe breathing difficulty, fainting, new confusion, sudden weakness, chest pain, or severe dehydration. Contact a healthcare professional for persistent, worsening, recurrent, or concerning symptoms.
