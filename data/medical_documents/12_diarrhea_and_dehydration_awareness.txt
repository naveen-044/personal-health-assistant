# Diarrhea and Dehydration Awareness
Category: Digestive health
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Diarrhea and general care

Diarrhea means loose or watery stools occurring more often than usual. Causes vary, including infections and other conditions. Replacing fluids is important, but the best approach depends on age, health, and severity. Follow local medical advice, especially for infants, older adults, pregnancy, or chronic illness.

## Prevention

Wash hands with soap and safe water, handle food safely, and use safe drinking water. Do not use anti-diarrheal medicines for a child or during severe illness without advice from a qualified professional.

## When to seek help

Seek medical advice for blood in stool, severe pain, high fever, repeated vomiting, signs of dehydration, or symptoms that persist or worsen. Confusion, fainting, inability to keep fluids down, or severe weakness needs urgent assessment.
