# Constipation Awareness
Category: Digestive health
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## What constipation can mean

Constipation can involve infrequent bowel movements, hard stools, straining, or a feeling that the bowel has not emptied. Normal bowel habits vary. Causes can include diet, activity, medicines, or health conditions, so persistent symptoms deserve assessment.

## General precautions

For many people, regular movement, adequate dietary fibre, and fluids suited to their health can support bowel function. Increase dietary changes gradually. Anyone with fluid restrictions, kidney or heart disease, pregnancy, or medicine-related symptoms should ask a clinician before major changes or laxative use.

## When to seek help

Seek urgent advice for severe abdominal pain, vomiting, blood in stool, inability to pass gas, or sudden bowel changes with marked illness. Discuss persistent constipation or new symptoms with a healthcare professional.
