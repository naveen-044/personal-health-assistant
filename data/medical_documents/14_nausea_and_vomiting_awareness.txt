# Nausea and Vomiting Awareness
Category: Digestive health
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## General information

Nausea is an unsettled feeling in the stomach; vomiting is forceful emptying of stomach contents. Many conditions and medicines can cause them. Symptoms alone cannot identify the cause.

## General precautions

Small, frequent sips of fluid may be easier for some people to tolerate, but severe illness or medical conditions can change what is appropriate. Do not give medicines to a child or combine anti-nausea products without advice from a clinician or pharmacist.

## When to seek help

Get urgent care for blood or coffee-ground material in vomit, severe abdominal pain, confusion, fainting, severe dehydration, or inability to keep fluids down. Seek professional advice for persistent or recurrent vomiting, or if the person is an infant, older adult, pregnant, or has a serious chronic condition.
