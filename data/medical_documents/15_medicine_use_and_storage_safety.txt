# Safe Medicine Use and Storage
Category: Medication awareness
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Using medicines safely

Medicines include prescriptions, over-the-counter products, and supplements. Read the current label or leaflet and follow the directions from the prescriber or pharmacist. Check active ingredients to avoid accidentally taking the same ingredient in more than one product. Do not share medicines, use someone else's prescription, or change a prescribed treatment based on chatbot advice.

## Interactions and storage

Tell a healthcare professional about all medicines and supplements you use. Ask before combining products, especially when pregnant, treating a child, or living with a chronic condition. Keep medicines in their original packaging and follow the storage instructions; store them securely away from children and pets. Dispose of expired or unused medicines using local pharmacy or public-health guidance.

## When to seek help

Ask a pharmacist or clinician about side effects, missed doses, or safe use. For suspected overdose, severe allergic reaction, trouble breathing, or collapse, contact emergency services or a local poison information service immediately.
