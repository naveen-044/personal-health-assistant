# Antibiotic Awareness
Category: Medication awareness
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## What antibiotics do

Antibiotics treat certain bacterial infections; they do not treat viruses such as the common cold or flu. A healthcare professional decides whether an antibiotic is appropriate. The right medicine depends on the infection and the person.

## Safe use

Use antibiotics only when prescribed for you and follow the prescriber's and pharmacy label instructions. Do not share, save for later, or use leftover antibiotics. Ask the pharmacist or prescriber what to do if a dose is missed or side effects occur. Do not stop or change a course without professional advice.

## Prevention and when to seek help

Vaccination when recommended, hand hygiene, and staying home when ill can help prevent some infections. Seek urgent help for breathing difficulty, facial or throat swelling, collapse, or other severe reaction after a medicine. Ask a clinician if symptoms worsen or do not improve as expected.
