# Over-the-Counter Pain Relief Safety
Category: Medication awareness
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## General information

Over-the-counter pain relievers include different medicines with different risks. Some products contain acetaminophen (also called paracetamol in many countries); others are nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs), such as ibuprofen or naproxen. Combination cold and pain products may share ingredients.

## Precautions

Read the product label and do not exceed its directions. Check with a pharmacist or clinician before combining pain medicines or using them with other prescriptions, supplements, pregnancy, kidney or liver problems, stomach ulcers, blood thinners, or chronic illness. Children need age-appropriate professional advice. This document does not provide doses or recommend a specific medicine.

## When to seek help

Seek urgent help for suspected overdose, severe allergic symptoms, vomiting blood, black stools, or breathing difficulty. Persistent or severe pain should be assessed rather than repeatedly self-treated.
