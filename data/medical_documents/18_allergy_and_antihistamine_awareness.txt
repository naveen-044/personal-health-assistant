# Allergy and Antihistamine Awareness
Category: Allergy and medication awareness
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Allergies

An allergy is an immune response to a substance that is usually harmless to most people. Possible symptoms include sneezing, a runny nose, itching, rash, or swelling. Triggers and severity vary, and symptoms do not establish an allergy diagnosis.

## Antihistamine precautions

Antihistamines are medicines used for some allergy symptoms, but products differ and can cause side effects such as drowsiness. Read the label and ask a pharmacist or clinician before use, particularly for children, pregnancy, older adults, or people taking other medicines. Do not drive or use machinery if a medicine makes you drowsy.

## Emergency warning signs

Sudden swelling of the tongue or throat, difficulty breathing, collapse, or a rapidly worsening reaction may be anaphylaxis. Contact emergency services immediately and follow any clinician-provided emergency plan.
