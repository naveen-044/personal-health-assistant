# Vitamins and Minerals Awareness
Category: Nutrition
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## General information

Vitamins and minerals support normal body functions. Needs differ by age, diet, health, pregnancy, and medicines. A varied eating pattern can provide many nutrients, but a clinician may recommend assessment or supplementation in some circumstances.

## Supplement precautions

Supplements can have side effects and interact with medicines. High amounts of some vitamins or minerals can be harmful. Do not use supplements to treat symptoms or a suspected deficiency without appropriate advice. Ask a clinician or pharmacist before starting a supplement, especially during pregnancy or when managing a chronic condition.

## When to seek help

Discuss persistent symptoms or concerns about deficiency with a healthcare professional rather than self-diagnosing. Severe or rapidly worsening symptoms need prompt assessment.
