# Food Safety Awareness
Category: Infection prevention
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Preventing foodborne illness

Food can carry germs that cause illness. Basic food safety includes keeping hands and food-contact surfaces clean, separating raw foods from ready-to-eat foods, cooking foods safely, and promptly refrigerating perishable foods. Follow local food-safety guidance because safe handling and cooking depend on the food.

## General precautions

Use safe water and clean utensils. Avoid food that appears spoiled or has been stored unsafely. People who are pregnant, very young, older, or immunocompromised may need additional precautions; ask a healthcare professional about personal risks.

## When to seek help

Seek professional advice for severe or persistent vomiting or diarrhea, blood in stool, high fever, dehydration, or illness in a higher-risk person. Confusion, fainting, or inability to keep fluids down requires urgent assessment.
