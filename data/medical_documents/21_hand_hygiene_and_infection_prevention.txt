# Hand Hygiene and Infection Prevention
Category: Disease prevention
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Hand hygiene

Handwashing with soap and clean running water helps remove germs. Wet hands, lather all hand surfaces, scrub, rinse, and dry with a clean method. If soap and water are not available, a suitable alcohol-based hand sanitizer can be used when hands are not visibly dirty; follow its label and keep it away from children.

## Other prevention steps

Cover coughs and sneezes, avoid close contact when ill where practical, and follow current vaccination and local public-health recommendations. Cleaning and ventilation can also help reduce the spread of some respiratory infections.

## When to seek help

Good hygiene does not prevent every infection. Seek healthcare advice for severe, persistent, or worsening symptoms, and urgent help for severe breathing problems, confusion, fainting, or other emergency signs.
