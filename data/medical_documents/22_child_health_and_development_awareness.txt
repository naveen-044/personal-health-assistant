# Children's Health and Development Awareness
Category: Child health
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## General child health

Children grow and develop at different rates. Developmental milestones are general guides, not diagnostic tests. A parent or caregiver who has concerns can discuss them with the child's healthcare professional, who can review development and arrange screening or support when appropriate.

## Prevention and medicines

Use age-appropriate health advice, routine preventive care, safe sleep and hygiene practices, and vaccination guidance from local health authorities. Never give a child an adult medicine or estimate a dose from chatbot advice. Check with a pediatric clinician or pharmacist before giving medicines or supplements.

## When to seek help

Seek urgent help for difficulty breathing, blue or grey lips, unusual unresponsiveness, seizure, severe dehydration, or rapidly worsening illness. Contact a healthcare professional for persistent fever or symptoms that concern the caregiver.
