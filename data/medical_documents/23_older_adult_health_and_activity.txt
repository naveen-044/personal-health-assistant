# Older Adult Health and Activity Awareness
Category: Older adult health
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## General health awareness

Health needs and abilities vary widely among older adults. Regular contact with a healthcare professional can support preventive care and review of ongoing conditions and medicines. Do not stop, combine, or change medicines without professional advice.

## Safe activity

Physical activity can support health at many ages. The type and amount that is safe depends on a person's abilities and health conditions. CDC guidance includes older adults and encourages aerobic activity, muscle strengthening, and balance activity when appropriate. Begin gradually and ask a clinician about new activity if there are health concerns, falls, chest symptoms, or significant mobility limitations.

## When to seek help

Seek urgent assessment for a fall with serious injury, sudden weakness, confusion, chest pain, fainting, or breathing difficulty. Discuss repeated falls, dizziness, memory concerns, or medicine side effects with a healthcare professional.
