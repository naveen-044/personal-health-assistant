# Mental Wellness Awareness
Category: Mental health
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## General wellbeing

Emotional wellbeing can be affected by stress, sleep, relationships, physical health, and life events. Feeling worried, low, or overwhelmed at times does not by itself establish a mental health condition. Support may include trusted people, community resources, and professional care.

## General precautions

Regular routines, social connection, rest, and enjoyable activity may support wellbeing, but they are not substitutes for treatment when needed. A mental health professional or primary care clinician can help assess persistent symptoms. The chatbot cannot diagnose or provide crisis care.

## When to seek help

Seek professional support if distress persists, interferes with daily life, or feels difficult to manage. If someone may harm themselves or another person, or cannot stay safe, contact local emergency services or a crisis line now and involve a trusted person if possible.
