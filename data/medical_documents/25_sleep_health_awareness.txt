# Sleep Health Awareness
Category: Sleep and wellbeing
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Why sleep matters

Sleep supports health and daily functioning. Sleep needs and patterns vary with age, health, and circumstances. Trouble sleeping can be related to stress, routines, medicines, pain, or sleep disorders.

## General sleep habits

A regular sleep and wake schedule, a comfortable and quiet sleep space, and limiting stimulating activities close to bedtime may help some people. Avoid using alcohol or unprescribed medicines as sleep treatments. Ask a healthcare professional before changing medicines or using sleep supplements.

## When to seek help

Discuss persistent sleep problems, loud snoring with breathing pauses, or daytime sleepiness that affects safety with a clinician. Seek urgent help if sleepiness contributes to a dangerous situation, such as difficulty staying awake while driving.
