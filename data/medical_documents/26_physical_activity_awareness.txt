# Physical Activity Awareness
Category: Healthy lifestyle
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## General benefits and approach

Regular physical activity supports health. Some activity is generally better than none, and movement can be adapted to a person's abilities. Choose enjoyable, practical activities and increase effort gradually after a period of inactivity.

## Public guidance

CDC guidance describes activity recommendations for different age groups, including aerobic and muscle-strengthening activity for adults and balance activity for older adults. Recommendations are population guidance, not an individualized exercise prescription. People with chronic illness, disability, pregnancy, recent surgery, or symptoms should ask a healthcare professional what activity is appropriate.

## Safety and when to stop

Stop activity and seek urgent care for chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, or sudden weakness. Discuss persistent joint pain, dizziness, or concerns about starting exercise with a clinician.
