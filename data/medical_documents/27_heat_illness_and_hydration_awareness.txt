# Heat Illness and Hydration Awareness
Category: First aid and seasonal health
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Heat and hydration

Hot weather can affect anyone and may worsen some chronic conditions. Reduce heat exposure where possible, rest in cooler or shaded places, and drink fluids as appropriate for your health. People with heart or kidney conditions, fluid restrictions, pregnancy, or certain medicines should ask their healthcare professional about safe fluid and heat precautions.

## Possible warning signs

Heat illness can become dangerous. Dizziness, weakness, heavy sweating, nausea, confusion, fainting, or a very high body temperature in hot conditions warrants prompt attention. Move the person to a cooler place and seek medical help; do not delay emergency care for confusion, collapse, seizure, or severe symptoms.

## Prevention

Check on people at higher risk during hot weather, plan strenuous activity for cooler times, and follow local heat alerts. Do not rely on thirst alone in high heat, but also do not force fluids on someone who is confused or unable to swallow safely.
