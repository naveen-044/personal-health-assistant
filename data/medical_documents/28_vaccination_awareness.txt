# Vaccination Awareness
Category: Disease prevention
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## What vaccines do

Vaccines help the immune system recognize and defend against certain germs. Recommended vaccines vary with age, health conditions, pregnancy, travel, location, and previous vaccination history. A general chatbot cannot determine which vaccines an individual needs or whether a vaccine is suitable for them.

## General precautions

Use current schedules from local public-health authorities and discuss personal questions with a healthcare professional or pharmacist. Keep vaccination records where possible and ask what to do if a dose was missed. Tell the clinician about serious allergies, prior reactions, pregnancy, or health conditions before vaccination.

## When to seek help

Ask a healthcare professional about expected reactions and when to seek care. Difficulty breathing, swelling of the face or throat, collapse, or another severe reaction after vaccination requires emergency help.
