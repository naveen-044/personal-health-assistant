# Basic First Aid for Minor Cuts and Burn Awareness
Category: First aid
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Minor cuts and scrapes

For a small cut or scrape, rinse the area with clean water. Firm, steady pressure with clean gauze can help control bleeding. If blood comes through, add more gauze rather than removing the first layer. Seek medical care if bleeding does not stop, the wound is deep or gaping, dirt cannot be removed, or the injury was caused by a dirty or rusty object. Wound care and tetanus advice depend on the injury and vaccination history.

## Burns

Burns can be caused by heat, chemicals, electricity, sunlight, or radiation. Serious burns can damage deeper tissues and may cause swelling, blistering, infection, or shock. Do not rely on chatbot guidance to judge burn depth. Seek urgent professional care for a large or deep burn, burns involving the face or airway, chemical or electrical burns, or signs of shock.

## Emergency precautions

Call local emergency services for life-threatening bleeding, collapse, serious injury, or breathing problems. Follow dispatcher instructions and do not delay emergency care to search online. First-aid training can help people respond safely; do not attempt procedures beyond your training.
