# General Wellness and Nutrition
Category: General health and nutrition
Reviewed: 2026-11-09

## Balanced eating

A healthy eating pattern can include a variety of vegetables and fruits, pulses or beans, whole grains, and suitable sources of protein. Limit highly processed foods that are high in salt, free sugars, or unhealthy fats where practical. Nutritional needs differ between people and across life stages; a clinician or registered dietitian can advise about individual needs.

## Vitamins and minerals

Vitamins and minerals support normal body functions, but supplements are not a substitute for a varied diet or medical care. Taking high amounts of some vitamins or minerals can be harmful or interact with medicines. Ask a pharmacist or healthcare professional before starting a supplement, especially during pregnancy, with a chronic condition, or when taking prescription medicines. Do not use a supplement to treat a suspected deficiency without appropriate advice.

## Activity, sleep, and hygiene

Regular movement can support overall health. Choose activity that is safe and practical for the person's abilities, and seek professional advice before a major change if there are health concerns. Good sleep habits include a regular sleep routine and a comfortable, quiet sleeping space. Handwashing with soap and water and routine personal hygiene can help reduce the spread of some infections.
